Adding a New Project

Want to add another assignment to your journal? Follow these steps whenever you complete a new project.

  1. Open index.html on GitHub.
  2. Find the section marked PROJECT CARDS.
  3. Copy one complete article class="project-card" block.
  4. Paste it inside the project-grid section.
  5. Change the title, description, and assignment link.
  6. Create your new assignment page in the assignments folder.
  7. Commit your changes and refresh your website.
